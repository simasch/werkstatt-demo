# Vision

## Problem

The office has six meeting rooms and no overview of them. Rooms are booked on paper lists at the doors, so meetings
collide, and newcomers do not know which room fits a group of twelve or has a projector.

## Solution

A small web application in which the office manager keeps the list of meeting rooms, and employees book a room for
a time slot. The system refuses a booking that collides with another one.

## Users

- **Office Manager** — sets up and maintains the meeting rooms.
- **Employee** — books a room for a meeting.

## Out of scope

Calendar integration, recurring bookings, catering, and access control are out of scope for the first release.
